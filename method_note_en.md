# Method note: corrective reproduction of LoRA under two training budgets

The question is whether a small trainable adaptation preserves task performance and sensitivity to word order after input correctness is established. The backbone is a pinned RoBERTa-base checkpoint. For a frozen projection W, LoRA adds a low-rank update, ΔW = (alpha/r)BA, with A in R^(r×d_in) and B in R^(d_out×r). Each adapted projection therefore adds r(d_in+d_out) parameters. We adapt the query and value projections in all 12 encoder layers and also train the classification head. This head is included in every reported trainable-parameter count.

Three methods are compared: a frozen encoder with a trained classification head, full fine-tuning, and LoRA rank 8. Each method runs seeds 42, 123 and 456 under separate four-epoch and twelve-epoch schedules. The official MRPC training partition is split into 2,934 gradient-training and 734 internal-development examples; the latter selects the epoch by F1, then accuracy, then earliest epoch. The 408-example official validation partition supplies the final exploratory evaluation. Rank 2 and rank 16 are single-seed diagnostics, not a search for a universal optimum.

A reproduction correction precedes every interpretation. The former legacy tokenizer loader instantiated a backend with zero BPE merges in this environment. The replacement uses AutoTokenizer, checks vocabulary and merge provenance, and matches the raw pinned tokenizer asset on every MRPC example and every actual cached input tensor. Faulty-input results are retained only as diagnostic evidence.

The six long-budget full/LoRA models are selected on MRPC internal development and locked before any PAWS inference. PAWS-Wiki public test assesses difficult high-overlap paraphrase pairs. Balanced accuracy and class-specific recall are central because a model can predict paraphrase too often. No PAWS training or checkpoint selection is performed. The tokenizer has 128-token truncation; two of the 8,000 PAWS pairs exceed it, as recorded in the input audit.

This is a method reproduction and controlled extension, not a reproduction of every published table. The authors' MRPC script uses a different budget, sequence length, hardware arrangement, and an MNLI-adapted initialization. Our backbone initialization, smaller gradient-training partition, three seeds and fixed method-specific learning rates limit numerical comparability. A deterministic repeat on one machine does not establish reproducibility across GPU architectures or software versions. Bootstrap intervals condition on each fitted model pair; three-seed variability remains a separate descriptive result.
